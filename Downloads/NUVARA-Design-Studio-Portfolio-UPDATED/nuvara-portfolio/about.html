ABOUT THE STUDIO

Designing spaces
with meaning.

NUVARA is a design studio focused on spatial design, interiors, scenography and visual identity. The studio combines functionality with atmosphere to create clear, contemporary and memorable environments.

APPROACH

Quiet forms.
Strong ideas.

Our process starts with observation: how people move, how light enters a room, how materials age and how an identity can become physical.

We work from the first sketch to the final visual, developing concepts that are practical, expressive and coherent.
